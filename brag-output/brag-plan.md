# Brag Plan: Sonarora

## Planning rubric
1. **App:** A local financial decision-support prototype for small traders and people with irregular income, comparing stock purchases against essential bills.
2. **Strongest line:** “See the trade-offs before you buy stock.”
3. **Visual hook:** R600 in hand, a R500 bill, R250 of stock: purchase timing changes what can be paid.
4. **Real UI:** Money inputs, actual calculated risk comparison, the three option cards, Money Map and the smaller-purchase popup.
5. **Length:** Exactly 80 seconds, explicitly requested by the user; this overrides Brag’s short-video default.
6. **Tone:** Polished; calm, clear, practical South African product demonstration.
7. **Audio:** Warm steady instrumental bed, sparse quiet clicks and soft transition hits. No narration requested or generated.
8. **Share caption:** Sonarora makes the trade-offs visible before you buy stock. Compare purchase timing, essential bills and uncertain payments using your own numbers. Independent prototype; not financial advice.
9. **Flow:** Enter reported money and timing → compare three options and inspect one possible money path → check a smaller purchase using supplier and bill details.

## Visual identity and LOOK
LOOK: 1920×1080 / 30 fps; #f4f8f8 ground, #102d3b ink, #007d87 accent, #e8f3f4 secondary, #125461 quiet teal; Manrope Variable 750 display, Source Sans 3 Variable 400/650 body; 120px outer safe margin; 80px hero type, 46–64px scene titles, 30–36px support; actual UI captures framed with 2px #d4e0e5 borders. No fabricated dashboard or bank branding.
BANNED: generic SaaS claims, guaranteed safety, investment returns, recommendations, fake testimonials, animated equalizers, strobing, artificial model percentages, voiceover.

## Hook
“Stock today. Bills tomorrow.” A R600 cash amount encounters a R500 bill and R250 stock purchase. The hook asks “What changes if the payment arrives late?” rather than advising a decision.

## Facts and data
All money and model output shown come from the current app’s invented example. Buy now has 6.25% modelled essential-payment risk; the waiting and receipt-triggered options both show 0% in this example. Do not imply the tied options differ or promise safety. Relative days are not calendar dates. Sales values are before costs, not profit. No live accounts, names, addresses, credentials or local URLs appear on screen.
