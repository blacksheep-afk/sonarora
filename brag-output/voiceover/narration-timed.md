# Sonarora — ElevenLabs voiceover

Voice: Queen B. Model: Eleven Multilingual v2. Warm, clear English. Each clip is fitted to its scene; the original music-only video remains unchanged.

## 0.4–6.8s · 01-choice

You need stock for your business. You also have bills to pay. What comes first?

## 7.4–13.8s · 02-introduction

Meet Sonarora. Explore the trade-offs before you buy stock, using the numbers you enter.

## 14.4–24.8s · 03-inputs

Start with your cash and bank money. Add your bill, stock cost, and expected payment. Enter the payment day and choose how likely it is to arrive.

## 25.4–34.8s · 04-results

Your result shows the modelled chance of missing an essential bill. These are estimates based on your numbers. Zero modelled risk is not a guarantee.

## 35.4–45.8s · 05-comparison

Compare buying now, waiting until after bill day, or buying after the payment arrives. In this example, two options have the same modelled risk.

## 46.4–56.8s · 06-money-map

The Money Map follows one possible outcome. See when stock is bought, money arrives, and bills fall due. A missed bill stays unpaid in this model.

## 57.4–67.8s · 07-smaller-purchases

Could smaller purchases help? Open the purchase check. Add the supplier's rules, delivery costs, and bills still to pay, then check a smaller first order.

## 68.2–74.8s · 08-privacy

No bank connection. No payments. Nothing is saved. Your numbers stay in this session.

## 75.3–79.8s · 09-outro

Sonarora. See the trade-offs. Independent prototype. Not financial advice.
